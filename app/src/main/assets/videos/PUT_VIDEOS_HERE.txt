Place your MP4 video files here.

Naming convention:
  - Video filename must match the image name used in arcoreimg
  - Example: if your image was named "poster.jpg" in arcoreimg,
             your video should be "poster.mp4"

Video encoding tips for best performance:
  ✅ Codec:     H.264 / AVC  (fastest hardware decode on Android)
  ✅ Container: MP4
  ✅ Resolution: 720p or 1080p (avoid 4K for overlays)
  ✅ Bitrate:   2–8 Mbps for 1080p
  ✅ Audio:     AAC stereo (or mute in code: player.volume = 0f)
  ✅ Duration:  Any — looping is handled automatically

Example files expected here (based on default imageVideoMap):
  - poster.mp4
  - flyer.mp4
  - business_card.mp4
  - album_cover.mp4
